Guides for Everyone

Whether you need help or want to offer services - here's what you should know

For Customers

  • Create your account and complete your profile
  • Post a clear service request with timing & location
  • Browse verified helpers in your area
  • Chat safely inside the platform
  • Finalize details before starting work
  • Never share personal contacts too early

For Helpers

  • Sign up and complete Aadhaar verification
  • Add your skills, experience and availability
  • Browse open requests that match your skills
  • Apply to jobs that suit your schedule
  • Communicate professionally with customers
  • Keep all conversations on the platform

Helpful Tips

Follow these tips for the best experience on the platform

Be Clear & Specific

Mention exact requirements, timing, and location in your request. Clear posts get better responses.

Complete Your Profile

Profiles with full details and verification get more visibility and trust from the other side.

Stay on the Platform

Always communicate through Home Helper Finder for safety and to keep a record of conversations.
